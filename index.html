Treatments

A considered menu of aesthetic care

Each appointment begins with a consultation. Treatments are discussed individually so the plan suits you. Descriptions below are factual outlines of the services offered.

Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Muscle-relaxing injectable treatment for areas such as the forehead and around the eyes, following consultation and assessment.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic-acid based fillers used to add volume or definition to selected areas of the face, planned during your consultation.

Profhilo & Skin Boosters

Injectable hydrating treatments that support skin quality. Course structure is discussed at your initial appointment.

Advanced Skin & Facials

Microneedling, chemical peels and skin treatments aimed at addressing skin concerns, selected to suit your skin during consultation.

Thread Lift & PDO

PDO mono and cog thread treatments. Suitability and approach are assessed individually before any procedure.

Wellness & Vitamin Injections

Vitamin B12 and related wellness injectables, alongside other treatments offered at the clinic. Discussed during consultation.
